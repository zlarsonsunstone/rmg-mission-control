Getting Started

Mission Control is the team collaboration dashboard for the RMG VA market research response. It tracks tasks, deadlines, quality, and communication with gamification to drive engagement and on-time delivery. The VA submission deadline is February 20, 2026.

Log in with your email and the password provided by your admin. On first login, you'll create your character (choose a class that matches your role). Then you'll land on your personal dashboard showing your assigned tasks and priorities.

Points are earned for completing tasks, helping teammates, and contributing to the project. Key scoring:
  • Task completed on time: 25-100 points (based on priority)
  • Early completion: 1.5x multiplier
  • High quality (90+): +25 bonus
  • Perfect first-try: +50 bonus
  • Helping a teammate: +15
  • Late completion: 0.5x multiplier
  • Revision required: -10 per round

Tips
  • Use Focus Mode when you need to eliminate distractions and concentrate on your tasks.
  • Check priorities every morning — they update every 6 hours based on project needs.
  • Complete tasks early for a 1.5x point multiplier.
  • Use @mentions in comments to notify specific teammates.
  • Flag for help early — don't wait until the deadline.
  • Upload files to specific tasks rather than to the general library for better organization.
